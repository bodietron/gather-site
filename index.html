Bring your intentions.
Make room for prayer.

Gather helps Christians across traditions share prayer intentions and support one another with a simple “I prayed.”

A caring place to gather

Choose to share with signed-in members, people in an approximate nearby area, or a private group. Post with your display name or anonymously, and keep prayers in your private Saved list.

Reading intentions requires an account. There are no comments or direct messages. Prayer counts are acknowledgments of care, never a measure of faith or worth.

Share thoughtfully

Gather’s English-language beta is for adults 18 and older. Submitted intentions, edits, updates, and group names go through automated moderation, with tools to report content and request review.

Anonymous posting hides your display name from other members. Your words can still identify you, and members may copy or screenshot them. Avoid sharing someone else’s private information.

Support and account help

For account access, feedback, or privacy questions, email gather@bodielab.com.

Accounts using email sign-in can request a password-reset email. Older accounts need to connect email recovery in Settings while they can still sign in. For account deletion, open Settings → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, contact support for help; please do not email passwords or sensitive prayer text.

Gather is currently in beta. This page does not provide public access to member intentions or a public download invitation.

When you need urgent help

Gather is not an emergency or crisis service, and reports are not monitored continuously. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby.
